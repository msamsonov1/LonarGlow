01

Modified Paschen Law in Lunar Degassing Voids

Pressure-Distance scaling for cable terminations

In ambient lunar ultra-high vacuum ($P < 10^{-10}\text{ Torr}$), mean free paths $\lambda_{mfp} \gg d$, suppressing avalanche breakdown. However, outgassing from polymeric joint potting or volatile lunar regolith creates micro-void pressures ($10^{-2}\text{ to }1\text{ Torr}$) right at the critical Paschen breakdown minimum.

$$V_B = \frac{B \cdot (P \cdot d)}{\ln[A \cdot (P \cdot d)] - \ln\left[\ln\left(1 + \frac{1}{\gamma_{se}}\right)\right]}$$

Where $A$ and $B$ are gas composition constants (primarily CO, $\text{CO}_2$, and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ outgassing), $d$ is joint gap separation, and $\gamma_{se}$ is the second Townsend secondary electron emission coefficient under high electric field stress $\mathbf{E} > 3\times 10^6 \text{ V/m}$.

02

High-Frequency Admittance Inversion: $(f, G)$

Spectral precursor signature prior to macro-arc flashover

Corona pre-breakdown produces localized Townsend micro-pulsing bursts ($10\text{--}100\text{ kHz}$) before bulk DC current escalates. By probing line admittance $Y(\omega) = G(\omega) + j\omega C(\omega)$, the real high-frequency conductance $G(\omega)$ reveals active micro-plasma charge transport.

$$G(f) = \text{Re}[Y(f)] = \frac{\pi f \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r'' |\mathbf{E}|^2}{\int |\mathbf{E}|^2 dV} + \sum_{k=1}^N \frac{q_e \mu_e n_e(k)}{1 + (2\pi f \tau_c)^2}$$

Where $n_e(k)$ is the electron avalanche density in the $k$-th joint micro-void, $\tau_c$ is ion-neutral momentum transfer relaxation time, and $\varepsilon_r''$ represents localized loss-factor degradation from carbonized tracking.

03

Regolith Dielectric Triple-Junction Enhancement

Electrostatic flashover mechanics on exposed transmission lines

Lunar regolith contains fine grains ($< 20\,\mu\text{m}$) rich in anorthite, pyroxene, and submicroscopic metallic iron ($\text{Fe}^0$). When deposited on cable insulation, particles charge under solar UV photoemission and HVDC fringe fields, forming triple-junction stress concentrators.

$$\mathbf{E}_{\text{local}} = \beta \cdot \frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{r_{\text{cable}} \ln(R_{\text{ground}} / r_{\text{cable}})} \cdot \left[1 + 2\left(\frac{\varepsilon_{\text{regolith}} - 1}{\varepsilon_{\text{regolith}} + 2}\right)\right]$$

Field enhancement factor $\beta > 8.5$ at jagged grain contact asperities drops local vacuum breakdown thresholds by $82\%$, inducing rapid surface flashover if not detected and mitigated within milliseconds.
